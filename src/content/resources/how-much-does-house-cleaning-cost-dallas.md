**Short answer:** the price of a house cleaning in Dallas depends on five things: the size of the home, its current condition, the type of cleaning (regular, deep or move-out), how often you book and any add-ons. A reliable company gives you a quote based on those details before booking, not a surprise at the door.

Below is how we think about it at Chavez House Cleaning, so you know what to tell any cleaner when you ask for a quote.

## 1. Size of the home

Square footage matters, but the **number of bathrooms and bedrooms** matters more. Bathrooms and kitchens take the most time per square foot. A 1,200 sq ft Uptown condo with two bathrooms can take longer than a 1,400 sq ft bungalow in the M Streets with one.

When you ask for a quote, give:

- Bedrooms and bathrooms (including half baths)
- Approximate square footage
- Number of floors and any stairs
- Rooms you do not need cleaned

## 2. Condition of the home

A home that was professionally cleaned two weeks ago is very different from one that has not been cleaned in months, or that just went through a move or a remodel. That is why many companies, including us, recommend a **deep cleaning first** and regular visits afterward.

## 3. Type of cleaning

| Type | What it covers | When it fits |
|---|---|---|
| [House cleaning](/services/house-cleaning/) | Kitchen, bathrooms, bedrooms, living areas, floors | Regular upkeep or a one-time refresh |
| [Deep cleaning](/services/deep-cleaning/) | All of the above + fixtures, trim, baseboards, vents, blinds, built-up grime | First visit, seasonal reset, before hosting |
| [Move-in / move-out](/services/move-in-move-out-cleaning/) | Empty-home deep clean, inside cabinets and appliances | Lease end, selling or moving in |
| [Post-construction](/services/post-construction-cleaning/) | Construction dust and residue everywhere | After building or remodeling |

Deep, move-out and post-construction cleanings take more time than a regular visit, so they cost more.

## 4. Frequency

A home cleaned **weekly or biweekly** stays in better shape between visits, so each visit is faster. That is why recurring cleaning usually costs less per visit than booking one-time cleanings. See [weekly and biweekly cleaning](/services/recurring-cleaning/).

## 5. Add-ons

Common extras that add time: inside the refrigerator, inside the oven, inside cabinets, interior windows and wall spot-cleaning. Ask for them when you request the quote so they are included in the time and the price.

## What to watch out for

- **A price with no questions asked.** If nobody asks about bathrooms, condition or pets, the quote will probably change on the day.
- **No checklist.** Ask what is included. We publish ours on every service page.
- **No reviews you can check.** Look for recent Google reviews that mention punctuality and communication.

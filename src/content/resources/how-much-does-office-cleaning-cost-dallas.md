**Short answer:** office cleaning in Dallas is usually priced per visit or per month, based on the square footage, the number of restrooms and kitchens, how many times a week you need cleaning, the time of day and what is on the checklist. The only way to get an accurate number is a short walkthrough of the space.

## The factors that set the price

### Square footage and layout

Open floors clean faster than many small private offices. Carpet, hard floors and the amount of glass (partitions, entry doors, conference rooms) all change the time.

### Restrooms and kitchens

These are the highest-effort areas because they are disinfected every visit. An office with four restrooms and a busy break room needs more time than one with a single restroom.

### Visits per week

Most small offices in Uptown, Downtown and Deep Ellum book **one to three visits a week**. More visits per week usually lower the price per visit, because the space never gets far behind.

### Time of day

After-hours, early-morning and weekend visits are the norm, so cleaning does not interrupt your team. Tell the company your hours and building access rules up front.

### Checklist and periodic tasks

Daily tasks (trash, restrooms, kitchen, floors, high-touch points) are different from periodic tasks (baseboards, vents, interior glass, inside the fridge). A good quote separates them so you know what happens every visit and what happens monthly.

### Supplies

Most cleaning companies bring their own products and equipment. Consumables like toilet paper, paper towels and soap are usually supplied by the office.

## How to compare office cleaning quotes

1. **Same checklist.** Give every company the same list of tasks and frequency.
2. **Who shows up.** Ask if it is the same team every visit and who your contact is.
3. **Communication.** How do you report a missed item, and how fast is it fixed?
4. **References.** Look for recent reviews that mention reliability and punctuality.

**Short answer:** to give yourself the best chance of getting your full deposit back in Texas, leave the unit clean inside every cabinet, appliance and closet, along every baseboard and on every floor, and give your landlord a forwarding address in writing. Texas landlords generally have **30 days** after you surrender the unit to refund the deposit or send a written, itemized list of deductions.

*This is general information, not legal advice. Always read your lease.*

## What Texas law says about deposits

- **30 days.** Under Texas Property Code § 92.103, the landlord must refund the deposit or give a written description and itemized list of deductions within 30 days after you surrender the premises.
- **Forwarding address.** Under § 92.107, the landlord is not obligated to return the deposit or send the list until you give a written forwarding address.
- **Normal wear and tear** cannot be deducted. Dirt, grease and trash left behind usually can, which is where a good move-out cleaning pays off.

## Kitchen

- Inside and outside of every cabinet and drawer
- Inside the refrigerator and freezer
- Inside the oven, racks and broiler drawer
- Inside and outside of the microwave
- Stovetop, range hood and filter
- Backsplash, counters, sink and faucet
- Dishwasher door, seals and filter
- Floor, including along the baseboards

## Bathrooms

- Tub, shower walls, glass and tracks free of soap scum and hard-water spots
- Toilet inside and out, including the base
- Inside vanities, drawers and medicine cabinets
- Mirrors, fixtures, towel bars and exhaust fan cover
- Floor and baseboards

## Every room

- Baseboards, door frames and doors
- Light switches, outlet covers and door knobs
- Inside closets: shelves, rods and floor
- Window sills, tracks and blinds
- Ceiling fans and light fixtures
- Carpets vacuumed; hard floors swept and mopped
- All trash and personal items removed

## Before you hand back the keys

1. Take dated photos of every room after cleaning.
2. Attend the walkthrough if your landlord offers one.
3. Give your forwarding address in writing.
4. Return all keys, fobs and garage openers.

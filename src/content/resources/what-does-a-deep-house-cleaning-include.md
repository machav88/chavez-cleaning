**Short answer:** a deep house cleaning includes everything in a regular cleaning, **plus the places that collect dust and grime over months**: baseboards, door frames, vents, ceiling fans, blinds, window sills, cabinet fronts, light switches and fixtures. It also includes a detailed scrub of kitchens and bathrooms, including hard-water stains and built-up grease. Inside the oven, inside the refrigerator and interior windows are usually priced as add-ons.

Below is the room-by-room checklist we follow at Chavez House Cleaning in Dallas, Texas.

## Kitchen

The kitchen is where a deep clean makes the biggest visible difference.

- **Stove** cleaned inside and out: burners, grates, knobs and the front of the oven.
- **Dishwasher** cleaned inside and out.
- **Cabinet fronts** wiped down, including handles where grease builds up.
- **Counters, backsplash and sink** scrubbed; faucet polished.
- **Appliance exteriors**: stainless steel cleaned and polished without streaks.
- **Blinds and window sills** cleaned.
- **Baseboards, light switches and light fixtures** cleaned.
- **AC vents** dusted and wiped.
- **Floor**, including the pantry floor, vacuumed and mopped.

*Common add-ons:* inside the refrigerator, inside the oven and degreasing the cabinets.

## Bathrooms

- **Shower walls** scrubbed and **hard-water stains** removed from the glass. Dallas tap water is moderately hard and leaves mineral spots that a regular wipe does not remove.
- **Tub, toilet, sink and counters** scrubbed and disinfected, including the base of the toilet.
- **Mirrors and fixtures** polished.
- **Cabinet fronts** wiped down.
- **Exhaust fan and AC vents** dusted and wiped.
- **Baseboards and doors** wiped down.
- **Floor** cleaned, including corners and behind the toilet.

## Laundry room

- **Cabinet exteriors** cleaned.
- **Baseboards and light switches** cleaned.
- **Floor** cleaned.

## Bedrooms, living areas and the rest of the home

- **Ceiling fans and vents** dusted.
- **Baseboards, doors, door knobs and door frames** wiped.
- **Blinds, window sills and glass doors** cleaned.
- **Stair rails** wiped.
- **Light switches** cleaned and disinfected.
- **Main entry door** wiped down.
- **All floors** vacuumed or swept and mopped: hardwood, tile, stone, vinyl and more.
- **Furniture and surfaces** dusted; **beds made** (linens changed when provided).

## Deep cleaning vs. regular cleaning, side by side

| Area | Regular cleaning | Deep cleaning |
|---|---|---|
| Counters, sinks, toilets, floors | Yes | Yes, more detailed |
| Baseboards and door frames | No | Yes |
| Vents, ceiling fans, light fixtures | No | Yes |
| Blinds and window sills | No | Yes |
| Cabinet fronts | Spot-clean | Fully wiped |
| Hard-water stains on glass | Light | Scrubbed |
| Inside oven / refrigerator | No | Add-on |

## How long does a deep cleaning take?

A deep cleaning takes longer than a regular visit. It depends on the size of the home, the number of bathrooms, how long it has been since the last professional cleaning, and any add-ons. A home that has not been cleaned in months, or one with pets or heavy cooking, needs more time. We tell you what to expect when we prepare your estimate.

## When should you book a deep cleaning?

- **Before starting recurring service.** It sets a clean baseline so every regular visit after that stays fast.
- **Once or twice a year**, as a seasonal reset (spring, or before the holidays).
- **Before hosting** family or an event.
- **When moving in or out.** For an empty home, see [move-in and move-out cleaning](/services/move-in-move-out-cleaning/).
- **After a remodel.** Construction dust needs [post-construction cleaning](/services/post-construction-cleaning/).

## How to prepare your home

1. **Pick up clutter** from counters, floors and tables so every surface can be reached.
2. **Tell us about pets**, alarms, parking and access.
3. **Point out priorities**: the shower glass, the stove, a room you want done first.
4. **Mention sensitivities** to products, or surfaces that need special care (marble, original wood).

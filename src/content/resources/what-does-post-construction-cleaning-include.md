**Short answer:** post-construction cleaning removes the fine dust and residue left after building or remodeling (on walls, trim, inside cabinets, in vents, on windows and floors) plus stickers, light paint specks and adhesive, so the space is ready for handover or move-in. It does not include hauling debris.

## Why it is different from a regular cleaning

Drywall and sanding dust is extremely fine. It settles on every horizontal surface, gets inside cabinets and drawers, and is pulled into vents and HVAC returns. If it is only wiped from visible surfaces, it keeps coming back for weeks.

## The usual phases

### 1. Rough clean (optional, during the project)

After major work is done and debris is hauled away: remove the bulk of the dust so the trades can finish.

### 2. Final clean (before handover)

The detailed clean that gets the space ready:

- Walls, trim, baseboards and door frames vacuumed and wiped
- Inside cabinets, drawers, closets and shelves
- Vent covers, ceiling fans and light fixtures
- Window sills, tracks and interior glass
- Stickers, labels, light paint specks and adhesive residue removed
- Counters, sinks, fixtures and appliances cleaned and polished
- Floors vacuumed and mopped with the right product for each surface

### 3. Touch-up (optional)

A quick pass right before the client walkthrough or move-in day to catch dust that settled after the final clean.

## What is not included

- Hauling debris, leftover materials or dumpsters (that is the contractor's job)
- Paint or grout repairs
- Heavy stains that need restoration, not cleaning

## Tips for homeowners and contractors in Dallas

- Schedule the final clean **after all trades are finished**, including the last paint touch-ups.
- Replace or clean HVAC filters after the dusty work, so the system does not spread dust again.
- In older East Dallas homes, remodeling one room spreads dust through the whole house. Plan to clean beyond the remodeled area.
